Our Story

A little house built on big flavor

Brew & Bliss began in 2013 with a second-hand roaster, one stubborn espresso machine and a simple belief: great coffee should feel like a warm hug. We source our beans directly from smallholder farms in Ethiopia, Colombia and Sumatra — roasted in tiny batches every morning before the sun is fully up.

Today, our cozy corner barista team serves more than 300 cups a day, remembers your order, and still treats every cup like it's the first one we ever made.
